# Start here — play Essey in 5 minutes

Essey is an on-chain stock-market club on **Robinhood Chain** (testnet). Open Cases to win real tokenized
stock, own a Seat and earn a cut of the club's fees, and move any of it privately. Everything below is
**testnet with play money — the tokens have no real value, so you can't lose anything.** Not financial advice.

The fastest path is the **Quest** on the [Start page](/start) — it walks you through each step and checks your
progress live. Here's the whole thing in plain terms:

## 1. Get a wallet + connect
You'll need a browser crypto wallet like **MetaMask**. Install it, reload essey.xyz, and click **Connect** —
it'll offer to add / switch to Robinhood Chain testnet for you.

## 2. Get play money (free)
On the [Quest page](/start), grab a little **gas ETH** from the faucet first (every action needs a tiny bit),
then drip yourself free **$ESSEY** (you spend it to play) and **USDG** (the play-money stablecoin). This is the
one step everything else depends on.

## 3. Do the fun stuff
- **🎁 Open a Case** ([Cases](/cases)) — roll a provably-fair **multiplier** (0.65×–50×) that pays out in real
  AAPL stock. The odds are read live from the contract, so you can verify them. Like any multiplier game, an
  average roll returns less than it costs — it's for fun, on play money.
- **⬡ Buy a Seat** ([Exchange](/market)) — 1 of 2,222 memberships, each with its own on-chain wallet (a Vault).
  **Stake a Tier** on it at the [Bell](/bell), and when the Bell rings, the pot of club fees pays out to staked
  Seats **in real stock**. (Owning holds your spot; *staking* is what earns.)
- **⚖ Supply** ([Lend](/lend)) — supply USDG to the lending pool to earn interest.
- **🛡 Go private** ([Essey Private](/private)) — shield your USDG, AAPL/NVDA, or a yield position so your
  balance and amounts are hidden, and send it privately.

## 4. Earn your mainnet whitelist
Completing the Quest's steps on testnet earns you a **whitelist spot** for the mainnet mint. That's the point of
the testnet run: learn the club by using it, and arrive on mainnet already knowing how.

## The honest part
Testnet, play money, experimental. Payouts are protocol fees shared to Seat holders — a mechanical fee-share,
**not a dividend, not a yield promise, not an offer of securities.** Nothing here is guaranteed, and provable
isn't the same as risk-free. Come break it and tell us what's confusing.
