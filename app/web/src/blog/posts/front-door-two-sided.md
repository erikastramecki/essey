## Green means on chain. Grey means not yet.

The center of the new landing is a ledger of what's real and what's coming, one card each, and every card carries a stamp.

A green stamp means it's on Robinhood Chain mainnet right now and you can check it yourself. A grey stamp means it's designed and scoped and honestly not deployed. Not claimable, not live, not pretending. You can tell the difference at a glance, which is the entire point. I'd rather you see the grey stamps than have me hide them and hope you don't do the math.

**What's green today:**

- **$ESSEY.** The fixed supply, 8,888,888,888 tokens, minted once. No mint key, no admin. It's the chip you hold and a claim on the floor beneath it.
- **The floor, the reserve.** An adminless reserve of on-chain tokenized stock. You redeem pro-rata against whatever it holds, minus a 5% exit fee, and the backing only rises. No owner, no withdraw, no lever. It holds what it holds.

**What's grey, and labeled grey:**

- **Holder basket and claim.** Pick which stocks your share of the floor pays out in, then claim it each epoch. Designed and scoped, not yet on chain. Coming.
- **Lending.** Post your tokenized equities and borrow against them without selling. The engine is built; mainnet activation is ahead.
- **Shielded claim.** Hold and claim your stock without revealing amounts. Built and proven end to end, experimental, not yet on mainnet.

That's the honest shape of it. Two things you can use today, three things I'm telling you are coming instead of showing you a mockup and letting you assume.
